FINAI Transformation

AI is Reshaping How
We Do Business

Our FINAI transformation is no longer a set of pilots. It is reshaping Bajaj Finance's business model and the way our customers experience us and is positively impacting business outcomes. This means structurally using AI across the board, in every process and across the entire lifecycle of the customer, from leveraging data for AI, using it in product and service discovery, customer onboarding and engagement, to using it at point of sale and branches. AI is now embedded in how we acquire, serve and grow with every customer.
