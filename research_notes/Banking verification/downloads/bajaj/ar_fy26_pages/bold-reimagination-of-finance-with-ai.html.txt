Bold Reimagination of Finance with AI Our Blueprint for Long-term Value Creation AI is rewriting business models. Power is shifting from enterprises to consumers. At Bajaj Finance, we see this not as a disruption to navigate but as our moment to lead in the expanse of our product suite, customer franchise, technology deployment and risk management. FY2026 marked the launch of our Long-Range Strategy 2026-30, a five-year blueprint shaped for a defining moment in financial services. Our strategy rests on three priorities: Customer Centricity, Technology Leadership, and Lowest Risk Company in India. Customer Centricity As a customer-centric company that serves all needs of the customer, we focus on experience, long-term relationships, products and processes designed for customer satisfaction. 01 Our Approach Design Thinking Iterative process Solve unknown, ill-defined problems Product-centric to Customer-centric Customer needs Long-term relationships Customer satisfaction Our 2030 Ambition Reduce cost of acquisition Significantly improve long-term relationships and customer satisfaction Higher product-per-customer Reduce credit costs Higher CSAT Greater customer trust and loyalty Personalised experiences Grow disbursement Technology Leadership in India We aim to be a technology leader in financial services in India. This means a deep commitment to leverage technology as a catalyst to achieve goals and drive innovation. It is the ability to anticipate trends, envision transformation and reshape business models to make life simpler for consumers. 02 Our Approach Data for AI Expand intelligent data variables. Organise structured and unstructured data sets. Power AI-driven capability models. Consumer AI Consumer AI is the use of AI technologies designed to power everyday products and experiences that make life simpler, more personalised and transformational for consumers. So far, we have remained focused on building enterprise AI capabilities. With consumers embracing AI rapidly, we are reimagining our existing digital platforms and building a new Consumer AI platform from the ground up.
