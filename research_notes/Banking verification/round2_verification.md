- **Finding:** The document exists. It is the Reserve Bank of India (Commercial Banks - Fraud Risk Management) Directions, 2026, ref **RBI/DoS/2026-27/412**, DoS.CO.FMG.6/23.04.001/2026-27, dated **July 31, 2026**. It is 26 pages and signed by C. Saravanan, Chief General Manager. It takes effect immediately (para 2).
- **Document URLs:** the landing page is https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=13641&Mode=0 and the PDF is https://rbidocs.rbi.org.in/rdocs/notification/PDFs/412MD5B6CA712E156413BACDEA9DD5CF5D1EF.PDF.
- **Saved:** `downloads\round2\rbi_fraud\` has the HTML page and `extract_chIII_and_X.txt` (verbatim excerpts of the key paragraphs). The curl download of the PDF was an HTML challenge page, so it is not a usable PDF. The full text was read in the browser instead.
- **Applicability (para 3):** "banking companies (other than Small Finance Banks, Payments Banks, and Local Area Banks), corresponding new banks, and the State Bank of India". RRBs and AIFIs, which the 2024 Master Directions covered, now have their own separate 2026 Directions (for example RRBs RBI/DoS/2026-27/454; AIFIs /457; SFBs /421). I confirmed these titles from the RBI notification listing.
- **Does it replace the 2024 Master Directions?** Para 67 (PDF p.25): "With the issue of these Directions, the existing directions, instructions, and guidelines relating to Fraud Risk Management as applicable to Commercial Banks stand repealed, as communicated vide circular no. DoS.CO.PPG.66/11.01.005/2026-27 dated July 31, 2026." That circular (RBI/DoS/2026-27/221, https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=13663&Mode=0) says "the 628 circulars listed in the Annex ... are hereby repealed ... with immediate effect." **PARTIAL:** the 2024 Master Directions are not named in the Directions' text. The 628-circular Annex was not on the page or in its 1-page PDF, so I could not confirm that RBI/DOS/2024-25/118 (15 Jul 2024) is on the list. The wording ("existing directions ... relating to Fraud Risk Management as applicable to Commercial Banks stand repealed") shows the intent to repeal and replace. A secondary source (Legal500) says the 2024 Directions are superseded, but I did not rely on it.
- **EWS / red flagging paragraph numbers (Chapter III, PDF pp.9-11):**
  - Para 12: framework for EWS and RFA under the Board-approved policy.
  - Para 13: RMCB oversees it.
  - Para 14: RMCB approves the EWS indicators; TAT for alerts is "preferably not more than 30 days".
  - Para 15: RMCB reviews red-flagged accounts.
  - Para 16: the framework is subject to validation.
  - Para 17: the framework must provide for a system of EWS integrated with CBS (17(1)), timely remedial action (17(2)), periodic review of sanction and monitoring (17(3)), and effective use of CRILC and CFR (17(4)).
  - Para 18: upgrade EWS on an ongoing basis.
  - Para 19: loan accounts, quantitative and qualitative indicators.
  - Para 20: Data Analytics and MI Unit.
  - Para 21: an alert triggers an examination of whether to red flag.
  - Para 22: report accounts with exposure of Rs 3 crore and above on CRILC within seven days.
  - Paras 23-25: EWS for non-credit transactions, including real-time monitoring and mule accounts.
  - Related: para 29 (CRILC reporting within seven days of red-flagging) and para 31 (classification as fraud or removal of the red flag "ordinarily" within 180 days).
  - The definition of Red Flagged Account is para 4(6).
- **Mapping to the 2024 numbering used in `rbi_verification.md`:** 2024 para 3.1.1 corresponds to 2026 para 12 (framework and RFA), 3.2 to para 17, and 3.3.2 to para 20. This mapping is my inference from matching text and was not checked against a concordance.

The only analytics wording is the "Data Analytics and Market Intelligence (MI) Unit" (paras 20 and 25) and "dedicated analytics set up" (para 25). Para 24 requires "transaction monitoring ... on real-time basis or with a minimum time lag". Do not cite this document as an AI/ML requirement.

## 3. DPDP Rules 2025

**Status: RESOLVED** for the notification date, Rule 1 commencement clauses and the penalty figures. The two commencement dates for Consent Managers and main obligations are computed from Rule 1, not stated in the Rules. No amendment gazette was found; a negative search is not proof.

- **Document:** The Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II Section 3(i), No. 760, NEW DELHI, **THURSDAY, NOVEMBER 13, 2025**, Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology notification **G.S.R. 846(E)** (the Hindi-text extraction shows "...846()" and the English text is "Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025"). File ID: CG-DL-E-14112025-267650 (uploaded 14 Nov 2025).
- **URL (MeitY-hosted gazette PDF):** https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/53450e6e5dc0bfa85ebd78686cadad39.pdf. Saved: `downloads\round2\dpdp\meity_rules\r.pdf` and `r.txt`. The PIB release is https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2190014&reg=3&lang=2 (PIB dates the notification 14 Nov 2025 in its summary; the gazette is dated 13 Nov 2025). A PIB PDF copy is in `downloads\round2\dpdp\pib_static\`.
- **Notification date:** 13 November 2025 (gazette header).
- **Rule 1 (exact quotes):**
  - "(2) Rules 1, 2 and 17 to 21 shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette."
  - "(3) Rule 4 shall come into force one year after the date of publication of this Gazette."
  - "(4) Rules 3, 5 to 16, 22 and 23 shall come into force eighteen months after the date of publication of this Gazette."
- **Consent Manager provisions:** Rule 4 ("Registration and obligations of Consent Manager", with First Schedule Parts A and B) commences one year after publication, which is **13 November 2026** (date computed by me from Rule 1(3) and the 13 Nov 2025 publication date). Rule 4(1): "A person who fulfils the conditions for registration of Consent Managers set out in Part A of First Schedule may apply to the Board for registration". First Schedule Part A item 1 requires "a company incorporated in India" and item 4 a net worth "not less than two crore rupees". Note that Rule 4 is the registration rule, so applications open at that date.
- **Main obligations:** Rules 3, 5 to 16, 22 and 23 commence eighteen months after publication, which is **13 May 2027** (computed). These cover, among others, the notice (Rule 3), security safeguards, breach intimation, retention, children's data and Significant Data Fiduciary duties. Rules 1, 2 and 17 to 21 (including the Data Protection Board provisions) were in force from 13 Nov 2025.
- **Maximum penalties:** the Rules do not set penalties. They are in the **Schedule to the DPDP Act, 2023** (Act 22 of 2023, Gazette Extraordinary No. 25, 11 Aug 2023, section 33(1)): item 1 (security safeguards, s.8(5)) "May extend to two hundred and fifty crore rupees"; item 2 (breach notice, s.8(6)) "two hundred crore rupees"; item 3 (children, s.9) "two hundred crore rupees"; item 4 (Significant Data Fiduciary, s.10) "one hundred and fifty crore rupees"; item 5 (Data Principal duties, s.15) "ten thousand rupees"; item 7 (any other provision of the Act or rules) "fifty crore rupees". Item 6 is a breach of a voluntary undertaking (s.32), up to the penalty applicable to the proceeding. Act PDF: https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf. Saved: `downloads\round2\dpdp\act\a.pdf` and `a.txt`. Section 42 limits any Schedule amendment to at most double the original penalty.
- **Possible change to dates (not confirmed):** a Jan 2026 MeitY consultation proposed cutting the 18 months to 12 months (secondary sources only; for example the Chambers article). My search found no amending notification. If one exists, the 13 May 2027 date would change. Check the e-Gazette before citing 13 May 2027 as final.

## 4. RBI Digital Lending Directions 2025, paras 27 and 17

**Status: RESOLVED.**

- **Document:** Reserve Bank of India (Digital Lending) Directions, 2025, **RBI/2025-26/36**, DOR.STR.REC.19/21.07.001/2025-26, **May 8, 2025**. URL: https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=12848&Mode=0 (PDF: https://rbidocs.rbi.org.in/rdocs/notification/PDFs/36NT8C402BE7C2A349E0BFFF3C526668CD7A.PDF, not downloaded). Saved: `downloads\round2\rbi_dl\p.html` and `page.txt`. I read the text from the HTML page, as fetched on 6 Oct 2026. I did not check whether any later amendment changed paras 17 or 27.
- **Para 27 "Disclosure requirements" in full:**
  - "i. RE shall put in place a mechanism to ensure that LSPs with whom they have a DLG arrangement shall publish on their website the total number of portfolios and the respective amount of each portfolio on which DLG has been offered. The name of the RE may or may not be disclosed as part of disclosure under this provision."
  - "ii. Disclosure under para (i) above shall be made on a monthly basis, with the disclosure for any given month to be provided no later than seven (7) working days following the conclusion of that month."
  - Para 27 is in Chapter VI (loss sharing arrangement in case of default). Para 21 also refers to "Disclosure requirements as under para 27".
- **Para 17 "Reporting of DLAs to RBI" (what it requires):** the RE must (i) report all digital lending apps (DLAs) deployed or joined, its own or the LSPs', "either exclusively or as a platform participant", on RBI's Centralised Information Management System (CIMS) portal in the format of Annex I; (ii) update the list when DLAs are added or cease; (iii) have the Chief Compliance Officer (or a Board-designated official) certify the data is correct and the DLAs comply with all extant instructions; (iv) certify that DLAs link to the RE's website with product, lender, LSP and customer care information and the Sachet Portal link, and have a nodal grievance officer, with data collection and storage under paras 12 and 13, and DLA particulars disclosed on the RE's website under para 8(iv); (v) ensure correctness and timeliness, as the data will be published on RBI's website in an automated manner and "RBI shall not verify/ validate the data"; (vi) not let inclusion be presented as RBI registration, authorisation or endorsement; (vii) "RE shall ensure that the reporting in respect of all DLAs on the CIMS portal is completed by June 15, 2025."
- **Effective date, para 2(ii):** "These Directions shall come into force immediately except for para 6, which shall come into effect from November 1, 2025, and para 17, which shall come into effect from June 15, 2025."
- **Para 17 is about DLA reporting to RBI, not DLG.** Do not describe it as a DLG disclosure.
