Caveats on fetching:
- **Q1 FY27 transcript source.** I could not get the transcript from a Bajaj-hosted URL. Five guessed cms-assets file names returned a 17-byte "not found" text. The only copy I could download is hosted on MediaNama's own server. It is the standard company/JP Morgan transcript format, with the company header and "Page x of 22" footers. Treat it as a mirror of the primary document, not a Bajaj-hosted copy. To close this gap, find the same PDF in the BSE/NSE filing for 30 Jul 2026 or on bajajfinserv.in investor relations.
- **Q4 transcript text.** The transcript and the decks are Bajaj's own documents, so quotes from them are primary. The Q4 transcript text has some garbled speaker labels from the PDF layout.
- **Page references.** Slides are cited by printed slide number, with PDF page in brackets where it differs. I read the Q4 and Q1 FinAI slides as rendered images because the text extraction scrambled the tables. Transcript page numbers come from the "Page x of N" footers. A footer closes a page, so text that appears just before "Page 4" is on page 4, and so on. I treated transcript pages as approximate to one page.
- **Not fetched.** I did not fetch the Q3 FY26 deck or call. The "800+" baseline is therefore checked only through the Q4 deck's own slide 12 text and through MediaNama's reporting. I also did not fetch the MediaNama Q1 article's transcript through Bajaj's site, and did not check the AGM.
- **Observer Voice.** The Observer Voice article on Sanjiv Bajaj and Rs 5,520 crore timed out twice. I did not use it.
- **Web search summaries.** The summaries were unreliable. For example, one gave "442 AI voice bots" and "Rs 1,980 crore PL". I could not confirm either figure and ignored both.

## Claim 1: Q1 FY27 (call of 30 Jul 2026)

### 1a. Bots handle about 71% of DIY customer-service volume

**VERDICT: VERIFIED-PRIMARY**

- Quote (Q1 Call, p.5, Rajeev Jain): "On service, AI bots are now handling 71% of our DIY customer service volumes."
- Q1 Deck, FinAI update (2/5), slide 11, row 9 "DIY Customers servicing through AI Voice and Text BOTs": Q1 FY26 50%, Q4 FY26 72%, **Q1 FY27 71%**.
- Note: this is a share of DIY (self-service) interactions. It is not a share of all customer-service volume.

### 1b. About Rs 2,500 crore of bot-led disbursals

**VERDICT: VERIFIED-PRIMARY**

- Quote (Q1 Call, p.5): "On disbursements, AI, voice and text bots did INR2,500 crores, we will do INR11,000 crores INR12,000 in the full year this year."
- Q1 Deck slide 11, row 8 "Disbursal from leads generated by Voice & Text AI BOTs", Rs crore: Q1 FY26 761, Q4 FY26 1,895, **Q1 FY27 2,551**, YoY **235%**. The call figure of Rs 2,500 crore is rounded.
- Separate line, "Disbursal from leads generated through voice log processing": Q4 FY26 372, **Q1 FY27 517**. The call says: "...resulted in 4 lakh additional offers and delivered INR517 crores of disbursements just as a result of better insight into the consumer."
- Note: the figure is disbursal from leads generated by the bots, not loans fully originated and approved by a bot.

### 1c. Voice bots are about 17-18% of loan originations

**VERDICT: PARTIAL**

- Quote (Q1 Call, p.9, Rajeev Jain, answering an analyst's question on AI contribution): "So, let's say, in a given month, if we do INR4000 crores to INR6,000 crores of urban and rural consumer PL, that's around INR6,000-odd crores between 17% - 18% is coming from voice bot. Voice bot and data put together, that would be 20-odd percent put together."
- Follow-up: Anurag Chottani says "The call center business is 20%." Rajeev then says "Of the call center business as Anurag has confirmed is 30%, data conversion, everything put together, you assume it is 20%. So just for PL," and the text ends at the page break. The transcript text here is garbled.
- Why only partial:
  - It is stated for consumer personal loans (PL) only, not for all loan originations.
  - It is an oral approximation for a "given month" ("let's say"). It is not a reported KPI.
  - The monthly base is stated loosely as Rs 4,000-6,000 crore.
  - The first answer ("17-18% from voice bot") sits next to "20-odd percent" for voice bots plus data.
- It does not appear in the Q1 Deck. The ~20% PL contribution is often reported alongside the 71% figure, so the MediaNama description is directionally right but over-generalised.

### 1d. Voice AI costs about 1/3 of a human agent

**VERDICT: VERIFIED-PRIMARY**

- Quote (Q1 Call, p.11, Anurag Chottani): "wherever the costs are involved, we make sure the cost of the AI is less than the cost of the human labor getting deployed. Like if I can talk about like voice AI will be like 1/3 of the human cost today, we run our voice model at."
- Rajeev Jain, same page: "Use case must be at 1/3, 1/5 the cost."
- The same "one-third" claim was made earlier in the Q4 Call (see below).

## Claim 2: Q4 FY26 (call and deck of 29 Apr 2026)

### 2a. About 72% self-service

**VERDICT: VERIFIED-PRIMARY**

- Q4 Deck, FinAI update (2/5), printed slide 10 [PDF p.11], row 9 "DIY Customers servicing through AI Voice and Text BOTs" (%): Q3 FY26 69%, **Q4 FY26 72%**, FY26 65%, FY27 assessment 73%.
- The Q4 Call transcript does not state 72% in words. Only the deck has it.

### 2b. Rs 1,895 crore of bot-generated disbursals

**VERDICT: VERIFIED-PRIMARY**

- Q4 Deck, printed slide 10 [PDF p.11], row 8 "Disbursal from leads generated by Voice & Text AI BOTs" (Rs crore): Q3 FY26 1,568, **Q4 FY26 1,895**, FY26 5,520, FY27 assessment 12,103.
- The Q1 FY27 deck restates Q4 FY26 as 1,895 on slide 11.

### 2c. Autonomous-agent target cut from 800+ to 600+ for FY27

**VERDICT: PARTIAL**

- Supports the cut to 600+:
  - Q4 Call, p.5, Rajeev Jain: "In FY27, we expect to deploy 600-plus autonomous agents and across operations, DMS, HR, technology and risk."
  - Q4 Deck printed slide 11 [PDF p.12], row "AI Agents": Q4 FY26 27, FY27 assessment "600+". Row "Agentic AI use cases", FY27: 118.
- Does not support a clean cut:
  - The same Q4 Deck, FinAI update (4/5), printed slide 12 [PDF p.13], item 3.2, still reads: "Scale Agentic AI capabilities by implementing **800+** autonomous agents across Sales, Operations, DMS, HR, IT and Risk" (FY27, "On track"). The deck is internally inconsistent: 800+ on the roadmap slide, 600+ on the metrics slide and in the speech.
  - Bajaj never says "reduced", "cut" or gives a reason. The "cut" is MediaNama's reading of the Q3 versus Q4 documents. I did not fetch the Q3 deck. The Q4 deck's own roadmap slide does show 800+, which supports the existence of the earlier number.
- The "800+" figure also appears in Q4 Deck slide 12 with "first 25 autonomous agents" completed in Q4 FY26 ("Completed (27 Agents)").

### 2d. Sales dropped from scope

**VERDICT: CONTRADICTED (as a standing fact). It is true only of the call wording.**

- Q4 Call wording: "operations, DMS, HR, technology and risk". Sales is not named.
- Q4 Deck slide 12 (800+) lists Sales.
- **Q1 FY27 Deck, FinAI update (4/5), slide 13, item 3.1**: "Scale Agentic AI capabilities by implementing 600+ autonomous AI agents across **Sales**, Operations, DMS, HR, IT and Risk" (FY27, "On track"). Sales is back in the written scope three months later.
- The Q4 omission is most likely a verbal list that skipped one function. No Bajaj document says Sales was removed.

## Claim 3: FY26 personal-loan figure Rs 5,520 crore and the 30 bp opex-to-NII improvement

### 3a. Rs 5,520 crore

**VERDICT: VERIFIED-PRIMARY (the number). The label differs across documents.**

- Q4 Deck, printed slide 10 [PDF p.11], FY26 column: "Disbursal from leads generated by Voice & Text AI BOTs" = **5,520** (Rs crore). It is not labelled personal loan in the deck.
- AR FY26, FINAI Transformation page, printed pp.14-15 [PDF p.12]: "**Rs 5,520 crore** Personal Loan disbursement via conversational bots". The same number sits in the "LRS FY2025-29 execution snapshot - FINAI outcome" table, printed p.40 [PDF p.35], as "Disbursal from leads generated by voice and text AI BOTs, C in crore, 5,520".
- The AR calls it personal loan, and the decks call it bot-lead disbursals. These are probably the same number, but I could not confirm that from a Bajaj statement.
- Related AR line (printed p.~46 [PDF p.56]): "over 52 million voice logs were converted into text, enabling C 697 crore annual disbursals." A separate line, "Company also transformed 2.3 million of underwriter personal discussion notes into structured datasets. This foundational shift strengthened underwriting quality and cut turnaround time during the year."

### 3b. 30 bp opex-to-NII improvement, and whether it is attributed to AI

**VERDICT: NOT-FOUND (30 bp). The attribution to AI is not supported.**

- The ratio Bajaj reports is **opex to NTI** (net total income). I found no "opex to NII" metric.
- FY26 improvement was **36 bps**, not 30:
  - Q4 Call, p.6, Rajeev Jain: "...opex to NTI I talked about it, showed an improvement of 36 basis points despite rapid expansion of gold loan branches, reasonable expansion of MFI branches by the firm last year."
  - Q4 Deck slide 27 [PDF p.28], "Management assessment - FY26 update": "Opex to Net total income improvement: What we said 40-50 bps, What we did 36 bps."
  - AR FY26 Table 7 (PDF p.45): consolidated "Total operating expenses to NTI 33.34% (FY26) vs 33.86% (FY25)", about 52 bps on reported figures. That differs from the 36 bps because of presentation and adjustment differences, which I did not reconcile.
- Not attributed to AI by Bajaj. The cited drivers are branch investment in gold loans and MFI. The Q4 Deck says only that FinAI "has begun to show early signs of costs and productivity benefits" (slide 9 [PDF p.10], no number tied to it).
- FY27 guidance of 25-40 bps (Q4 Call and Q1 Call) is stated alongside AI, but not as caused by it. Rajeev Jain, Q4 Call: "we'll probably need AI to be able to deliver that" (Sandeep Jain, in a profit-growth answer).
- Q1 FY27 opex to NTI was 33.4%, "marginally higher than last year" (Q1 Call, p.3), because of gold loan and MFI branches and the new labour code.

### Fraud

## Notes for the MBA write-up

1. The headline bot-disbursal numbers and the 71-72% self-service share are reliably primary-sourced. Use the investor decks as the citation, not the speech.
2. "Voice bots are 17-18% of loan originations" should be cited as an oral estimate for consumer PL only.
3. Do not attribute opex-to-NTI improvement to AI. Bajaj itself does not, and the FY26 figure is 36 bps.
4. For collections and early warning, Bajaj gives bot activity counts, not outcome metrics such as recovery or delinquency. That is a real gap worth stating in the paper.
5. Treat the 800+ to 600+ change as an inference. The Q4 deck contains both numbers, and the Q1 FY27 deck includes Sales again.
